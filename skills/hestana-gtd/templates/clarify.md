# Clarify an Inbox item

1. What is it?
2. Is it actionable?
   - No: cancel it, send it to Someday/maybe, or keep it as a document.
   - Yes: what is the outcome, and what is the next physical action, written with a verb?
3. One step or more? More than one: it is a project; create the subtasks and order them with prerequisites.
4. Two minutes or less: do it now.
5. Someone else's to do: move it to Waiting for and write who and since when.
6. Otherwise: move it to Next actions. Set the availability (people, actors or anyone).

An agent proposes this as a comment; the person decides.
