# Feature brief template

Use when the outcome needs discovery or coordinated results. A title or sentence is enough for initial capture; enrich progressively.

Replace braces with actual context and remove unused prompts. Keep unanswered consequential questions explicit. This is a writing aid, not a server schema or an authorization.

```markdown
# {Feature title}

Agreement: {draft/accepted reference and revision; who decided what, with provenance}
Methodology: {human-agent-development, the Space's version as read from spaces_get, skill hestana-had 0.7.0}
Context: {project/Space and current governing policy references}

## Problem and desired outcome

{Affected users, current difficulty, why it matters and observable improvement. Keep the original request distinct from interpretation where useful. State any success measure and its agreed measurement route; do not invent targets.}

## Scope and boundaries

{Included behavior and exclusions. State whether implementation, release or live validation is promised.}

## Inputs requirements and decisions

{Accepted requirements and directly retrievable evidence/specification references with revisions. Separate facts, assumptions and pending decisions.}
{For each material pending decision: local reference, question, affected work, authorized decision-maker if known, status and answer/provenance when resolved.}

## Acceptance direction

- {AC1: initial context + action/event + observable result}
- {Relevant failure/recovery case and expected result}
- {Relevant permitted/forbidden actor or space boundary and non-disclosure expectation}

## Proposed delivery and execution needs

{Smallest useful slice; contributions and actual required inputs. Distinguish group from executable work.}
{Required expertise/access/authority, accountable person if known, and unresolved routing. No new permission is granted.}

## Verification and acceptance route

{Map criteria to planned evidence and applicable quality/review policy references. Identify an authorized verifier/acceptor only where already designated; otherwise record the unresolved routing.}

## Current disposition and next action

{Unresolved scope/questions/blockers, what can proceed now and what must wait. Speculative work stays a proposal.}

## Result and handoff when work occurs

{Artifact/commit and exact contract references; observed verification outcomes and by whom; acceptance/release state, remaining work and concrete next action. Do not fill planned evidence as completed.}
```
