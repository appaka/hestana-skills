# Research work template

Use when the deliverable is an answer with evidence. A concrete evidence boundary can replace a timebox; do not invent a deadline, budget or positive conclusion.

Replace braces with actual context and remove unused prompts. This is a writing aid, not a server schema or an authorization.

```markdown
# {Question to investigate}

Question and purpose: {what decision or action this should inform}

Boundary: {sources/context to inspect, permitted experiments/actions, exclusions and stop condition}

Inputs and constraints: {relevant prior evidence, authorized access, source freshness and unavailable inputs}

Acceptance:
- {Question addressed with traceable sources/observations; inference and uncertainty separated}
- {Limitations and practical recommendation/next decision explicit}
- {Negative/inconclusive result handled against the agreed stop condition; inaccessible evidence is reported without disclosure/bypass}

Report and handoff: {fill after work: report reference/revision, evidence summary and gaps, reviewer/check outcome and current contract reference}

Next decision/action: {recommendation, designated decision-maker if known, unanswered questions/blockers; no automatic implementation or approval}
```
